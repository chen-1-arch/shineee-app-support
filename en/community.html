Community Standards

Safety rules for Shineee! Community, Fitness Ranking, friends, and interactions

简体中文

Purpose

Shineee! Community is for sharing genuine nutrition and training experiences and reviewable workout plans. Fitness Ranking compares training results and physique presentation against published criteria. Neither is a dating, attractiveness, sexual-appeal, health, or personal-worth rating service. Follows, friends, and encouragements are for low-pressure support between genuine training peers.

Who May Participate

  • You must be at least 18 years old before joining Ranking or using friend features.
  • Upload only photos of yourself that you have the right to share.
  • Use accurate, non-deceptive profile and training information.
  • Do not create fake users, friends, messages, rankings, or activity.

Prohibited Content

Do not submit nudity or sexual content, content involving or appearing to involve a minor, impersonation or stolen photos, harassment or hate, threats or violence, dangerous or illegal content, advertising or QR codes, AI-generated people, face swaps, materially distorted physique images, or content unrelated to fitness progress. Bullying, humiliation, discrimination, and objectification are prohibited.

Posts, Photos, Videos, and Workout Plans

Before posting, you must confirm that you have the right to share the submitted text, photos, and video publicly. Do not repost a fitness creator's avatar, footage, photos, paid material, or complete work without permission. When summarizing public training information, identify the original source, summary date, and authorization status. Do not imply an unconfirmed partnership or endorsement with phrases such as “official same plan.”

Photos or videos of up to 60 seconds and 50 MB are stored in account cloud storage. Posts enter review first and appear in Recommended or Following only after approval. A shared workout plan is another user's record or suggestion; review it against your health, ability, and recovery before adopting it. Adoption never creates a false completed-workout record.

Friend Chat and Encouragement

A non-friend may send one filtered text message. The sender cannot send another unless the recipient replies. A reply opens the conversation but does not automatically add either person as a friend. The recipient can ignore, report, or block; new conversations are rate-limited and an ignored sender must wait before trying again. Friendships and messaging permission are separate, and predefined encouragements are available only to friends or in an opened conversation. The first version does not support links, contact details, images, audio, or attachments. Do not use variants, codes, or other methods to evade these safeguards. Unfriending does not close an independently opened conversation; blocking ends chat access.

Sharing Today's Diet

Today's-diet sharing is off by default. If you turn it on, other signed-in Ranking users can see the genuine foods and nutrition summary you logged for that day on your profile. Turning it off clears the public snapshot, and the snapshot expires according to your local date. Do not fabricate records to mislead others or publish unrelated sensitive information.

Reporting, Blocking, and Enforcement

You can report a community post, Ranking content, profile, or friend message and can block another user. Reporting a post immediately hides it from the reporter's Community view and sends it for human review; message reports are likewise hidden from the reporter first. Blocking cancels follows and friendships, stops direct chat, and removes the relevant user and content from later interactions.

We may keep content hidden, reject publication, remove content, restrict feature access, or take another proportionate action. For added report context, an appeal, or a safety concern that is not resolved in the app, email 1021885833@qq.com. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Leaving and Deletion

You can delete your own posts and their photos or video, or leave Fitness Ranking and remove participation data. Permanent account deletion removes Community posts and media, follow relationships, Ranking photos and score, match history, friend relationships and messages, related reports, and the public diet snapshot. Deleted content cannot be restored.
