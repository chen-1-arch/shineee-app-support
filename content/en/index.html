Track progress with less friction

Shineee! helps people working on fat loss, muscle gain, and strength training log food and nutrition, estimate activity burn, follow body and strength changes, and use AI-assisted food recognition and progress analysis.

If AI does not return a result

Check your connection and try again. Shineee! does not invent an AI-style answer when the model times out or fails; the app will show a clear failure state.

Accounts and data

Shineee! requires an account. Records are isolated by account and cloud-synced so they can be restored on a new device. You can clear account data, sign out, or permanently delete your account inside the app.

Fitness Ranking

Adults age 18 or older may choose to submit their own physique photos. Photos stay hidden until AI review succeeds. Active approved participants can mutually confirm friendships, exchange text or predefined encouragements, and choose whether to share that day's genuine diet log on their profile. Participants can report content, block users, or leave Ranking and delete participation data inside the app. Ranking judges fitness presentation, not attractiveness or personal worth.
