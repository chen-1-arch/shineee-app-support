Community Standards

Safety rules for Shineee! Fitness Ranking, friends, and interactions

简体中文

Purpose

Fitness Ranking is an optional, adults-only space for comparing training results and physique presentation against published criteria. It is not a dating, attractiveness, sexual-appeal, health, or personal-worth rating service. Friend and encouragement features are for low-pressure support between genuine training peers.

Who May Participate

  • You must be at least 18 years old before joining Ranking or using friend features.
  • Upload only photos of yourself that you have the right to share.
  • Use accurate, non-deceptive profile and training information.
  • Do not create fake users, friends, messages, rankings, or activity.

Prohibited Content

Do not submit nudity or sexual content, content involving or appearing to involve a minor, impersonation or stolen photos, harassment or hate, threats or violence, dangerous or illegal content, advertising or QR codes, AI-generated people, face swaps, materially distorted physique images, or content unrelated to fitness progress. Bullying, humiliation, discrimination, and objectification are prohibited.

Direct Messages, Friends, and Encouragement

Friendship and messaging permission are separate. A non-friend may send one filtered text message and must wait for the recipient to reply before sending more. A reply opens the conversation without automatically creating a friendship. Recipients can ignore, report, or block. The first version does not support links, contact details, images, audio, or attachments. Friend requests, new stranger conversations, and messages have limits or waiting periods. Do not use variants, codes, or other methods to evade these safeguards. Unfriending does not close an independently opened conversation; blocking stops both reading and sending.

Sharing Today's Diet

Today's-diet sharing is off by default. If you turn it on, other signed-in active Ranking participants can see the genuine foods and nutrition summary you logged for that day on your profile. Turning it off clears the public snapshot, and the snapshot expires according to your local date. Do not fabricate records to mislead others or publish unrelated sensitive information.

Reporting, Blocking, and Enforcement

You can report content from a ranking card, profile, or friend message and can block another user. A photo or profile report hides the affected Ranking content as described in the app while safety review runs. Reporting a message immediately hides that message from the reporter's conversation view and records it for review. Blocking removes the friendship, stops direct chat, and prevents the other person from appearing in your future Ranking matches.

We review reports promptly and may keep content hidden, remove content, restrict feature access, or take another proportionate action. For added report context, an appeal, or a safety concern that is not resolved in the app, email 1021885833@qq.com. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Leaving and Deletion

You can leave Fitness Ranking and delete your participation data from the Ranking screen. Once deletion completes, Ranking photos, score, match history, friend relationships, friend messages, related reports, and the public diet snapshot are deleted together. Nutrition, training, and account data remain available. Permanent account deletion remains available separately in the app.
