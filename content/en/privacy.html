Privacy Policy

For the Shineee! iPhone app

简体中文

Overview

Shineee! is for nutrition, training, and body-progress tracking. We process only the data needed to provide these features. The app has no advertising or cross-app tracking, does not sell personal data, and does not use health or fitness data for targeted advertising.

On-Device and Cloud-Synced Data

Profile details, nutrition and activity logs, measurements and strength records, goals, preferences, saved meal plans, and AI chat history are separated by account on the current device and synced to that account's cloud storage so they can be restored after reinstalling the app or changing devices.

You can clear the current account's data inside Shineee!. Removing the app deletes the on-device copy but does not by itself delete cloud account data.

Account Data

Shineee! requires registration or sign-in. Depending on the method you choose, we process an email address, Apple-provided account identifier, or phone number, together with the app user identifier and profile name. Tencent CloudBase provides account and cloud-sync infrastructure.

  • Profile image and optional display name;
  • Age, sex, height, weight, activity level, and fitness goal;
  • Nutrition, activity, measurement, and strength records;
  • Custom foods, meal plans, food images, preferences, and AI chat history;
  • Ranking friend relationships, friend messages, predefined encouragements, unread state, message reports, and the today's-diet snapshot you choose to share.

AI Features and Explicit Permission

Before Shineee! sends personal data to third-party AI, the app explains what will be sent, identifies the recipients and purpose, and asks for explicit permission. If you decline, manual logging remains available. You can withdraw permission under “About & Privacy,” and the app will ask again before a future AI request.

When you actively request food recognition, nutrition or progress analysis, AI chat, or speech transcription, the request may include text you enter, photos or files you select, a voice transcript, and the nutrition, training, or body-progress context needed to complete the request.

These requests pass through Shineee!'s Tencent Cloud services and are sent to Volcano Engine AI services, including Doubao model and speech capabilities, only to complete the feature you requested. Raw microphone audio is not written to Shineee!'s business storage. A transcript you choose to send may be stored in your account's AI chat history.

Do not include sensitive personal information that is unrelated to the feature in an AI prompt, photo, or file.

Fitness Ranking Photos

Fitness Ranking is optional and limited to adults age 18 or older. Before upload, you must confirm the photos are yours, agree to their selected public visibility, and separately agree that the photos and competition profile may be processed by Tencent Cloud and Volcano Engine AI for safety and eligibility review.

Photos remain hidden if review fails or the model does not return a result. We may retain review status, reason, confidence, model, and rule version for safety auditing. Approved photos can be shown to other ranking users according to the visibility you selected.

Friend Relationships, Messages, and Today's Diet

Only active Ranking participants age 18 or older whose photos passed review can send or accept friend requests. Text messages and predefined encouragements become available only after both people confirm the friendship. The first version does not support anonymous chat, links, contact details, images, audio, or attachments. We store friend requests and relationships, message text and type, sent and read times, encouragement type, and limited send-deduplication and rate-control state to provide the inbox, chat, unread, and abuse-prevention features.

Text messages are subject to content filtering and rate limits. You can report a message you received; it is immediately hidden from your conversation view, while the report reason and status are stored for safety review. Blocking removes the friendship, stops direct chat, and prevents the other person from appearing in your future Ranking matches.

Today's-diet sharing is off by default. If you turn it on, other signed-in active Ranking participants can see the food names, portions, and nutrition summary you genuinely logged for that day on your profile. Turning sharing off clears the public entries from the server, and public visibility expires according to your local date. An expired snapshot that has not yet been updated remains private in cloud storage until your next update, until you turn sharing off, or until you leave Ranking or delete your account.

After unfriending, prior friend messages remain stored and unavailable while the two people are not friends. If both people later confirm the friendship again, the prior conversation may become visible again. Related social data is deleted when either person leaves Ranking or deletes the account. Do not include unrelated sensitive personal information in a friend message or shared diet entry.

Mainland China Phone Sign-In

Phone-number one-tap and SMS sign-in are regional options. They are not required for English-language use. If you actively use them, Alibaba Cloud Number Authentication and the applicable mainland China carrier may process the phone number, network and carrier information, IP address, device model, operating system, and SIM state needed for authentication. You may instead use email or Sign in with Apple.

International Processing

Shineee!'s current account, cloud, and AI service paths are operated in mainland China. If you use Shineee! from another country or region, data used for account sync or an AI request may be transferred to and processed in mainland China. Do not use an AI feature if you do not agree to that transfer; manual logging remains available.

Before expanding public operations in a country or region, Shineee! must also evaluate the local legal, transfer, retention, and user-rights requirements that apply there. This policy does not claim that every market has already been approved for public operation.

System Permissions

Camera and photo-library access are used for food photos, an account image, and fitness-ranking photos you choose to submit. Microphone and speech-recognition access are used only when you actively start voice input. Permissions can be changed in iOS Settings.

Retention and Deletion

On-device data remains until you clear it or remove the app. Cloud account data remains until you request account deletion. A signed-in user can permanently delete the account inside Shineee! under “Profile → Delete Account.” After deletion is completed, the account and related cloud data cannot be restored.

You can also leave Fitness Ranking without deleting the rest of the account. Once Ranking deletion completes, Ranking photos, score, match history, participation profile, friend relationships, friend messages, related message reports, and the public diet snapshot are deleted together. Nutrition, training, and account data remain available.

Withdrawing AI permission stops future AI sharing but does not automatically delete existing account data or chat history. Use the in-app data clearing or account deletion controls when you want those records removed.

Security and Providers

Data is transmitted using HTTPS. We limit processing to the feature being provided and require account, cloud, and AI providers to protect data consistently with this policy and applicable requirements. No online service can guarantee absolute security, and we continue to improve safeguards.

Health Notice

Calories, nutrition, activity burn, and AI analysis are for everyday tracking and reference. They are not medical diagnosis, treatment, or personalized medical advice. Consult a qualified professional for health or medical decisions.
